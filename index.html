Frequently Asked Questions

Is LPMS free?

Yes — during the beta period, LPMS is completely free. No credit card required.

Do I need to install anything?

No. LPMS runs in your browser — just sign up and start managing your properties.

What happens after the beta?

Beta users will have the option to continue using LPMS at a discounted rate or free for a limited time.

Can I manage multiple properties?

Yes. LPMS is designed for landlords and agents with any number of properties and units.
